About (?)

This is a fork of SUchedule, originally created by Adnan Burak Ayaz to make the course registration period easier for Sabancı University students. It is MIT licensed.

Original project:

github.com/aburakayaz/suchedule

This fork, maintained by Aziz Derin Ebil, adds a conflict filter, an undergraduate/graduate filter, multiple schedule plans and shareable schedule links:

github.com/azizebil/suchedule

Feel free to contact me about your suggestions:

aziz.ebil@sabanciuniv.edu

Thank You!
